Support

Email support@ownerswitch.com. We answer as soon as we can. Please do not send sensitive information.

Purchase and restore

Holiday Helper is a one-time Apple in-app purchase. Apple shows the price in your currency before you buy. To restore it on a new iPhone, tap Restore purchase on the welcome screen, on the purchase screen, or in the Parent menu. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments. Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

December doors

One door opens each December night, from December 1 to December 24. Earlier nights stay open. To see every night before December, open the Parent menu, answer the question, and turn on Preview any night.

Your data

Savored Roots has no account and does not use your photos. Your settings stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
