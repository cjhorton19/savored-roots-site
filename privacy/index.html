Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026.

Thorton LLC, a California limited liability company, runs Savored Roots. Address: 2108 N St Ste N, Sacramento, CA 95816. Support: support@ownerswitch.com. Privacy: privacy@ownerswitch.com.

The app

Savored Roots is for parents. We collect no data through the app.

No account is needed. There are no photos, no server storage, no live AI, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. Your settings, such as Preview any night, stay in the app on your device.

The app includes one built-in character and 24 pre-made scenes bundled in the app. The art is AI-assisted. The app does not send anything for AI processing.

Purchases

Apple handles payment for the one-time purchase in the free app. The purchase check happens on your device with Apple's system. We do not receive receipts or payment details. Apple handles its purchase data under the Apple privacy policy.

Email

The only data we receive is email that you choose to send to support or privacy. We use your email address and message only to answer you. Please do not send sensitive information. We keep these emails only as long as we need them to help you. You can ask us to delete them at privacy@ownerswitch.com.

Changes

We store nothing from the app on servers. If a future version needs to store data or use a photo, we will update this policy and explain the new use before you choose that feature. We will never silently start collecting photos.
