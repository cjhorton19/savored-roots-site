Savored Roots

A December ritual for parents. Meet Sedgepuff, an original pig and your holiday helper. Open one door each December night, see where Sedgepuff went, and read a short bedtime note aloud.

The app is free to download. Holiday Helper is one in-app purchase that unlocks all 24 scenes for December 1–24. There is no subscription and no account.
